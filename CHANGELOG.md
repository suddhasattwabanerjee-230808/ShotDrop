# Changelog

## [0.8.0] - 2026-10-24 (build 17)

People, sharing, and sideways photos turned upright.

- **People filter in both galleries.** A face button in the top bar opens People: everyone ShotDrop has found in two photos or more, as round faces with how many photos each is in. Tap someone to see only their photos (it works together with the date filter); their face sits in the top bar, ✕ shows everyone again. Faces are found on the phone while ShotDrop is open, newest photos first, and remembered, so each photo is looked at once. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Share from Selected.** Long-press photos in Selected and tap Share for Android's own share sheet: WhatsApp, Messages, Bluetooth, Drive and every other app that takes photos. The edited version goes where there is one.
- **Sideways people turned upright.** A landscape photo with a person lying sideways (camera held upright, but saved landscape) shows in portrait in the galleries and the viewer, turned whichever way stands them up (usually left 90°). Selected, the editor and sharing get it upright too; the editor's turn button still turns it back. Real landscape shots stay as they are.

Little cartoon friends around the app. Appearance only, as before.

- **Props in the background.** A lion, a kite, a smiling cloud, a butterfly, a balloon, a bird and a paper plane sit round the edges of every tab, among the confetti.
- **A lion peeking over the tab bar**, paws on its edge. Every so often it pops up and tilts its head.
- **A kite flying off the bouncing picture** on the empty and waiting screens.
- **A cheering lion and balloons** with the tick when posting is done.
- **A lion flying a kite under a rainbow** at the bottom of Settings: Happy Shooting!

## [0.7.0] - 2026-10-22 (build 15)

A playful new look. Only the appearance changes: every screen works exactly as before.

- **Candy colours and a rounded font.** Bubblegum pink is the main colour, with a rainbow alongside it (tangerine, sunny yellow, grass green, sky blue, grape). Text is set in Fredoka, a round, friendly typeface.
- **Dark and light both play.** Dark is a grape night sky; light is a warm cream playroom. The sun / moon button is now a yellow or purple bubble and spins as it switches.
- **Confetti behind the tabs.** Soft colour glows and a sprinkle of dots, rings, sparkles and squiggles sit behind the galleries, Selected, Upload, Settings and setup.
- **A tab bar with a colour per tab.** SD Card is orange, Phone blue, Selected pink, Upload green, Settings purple; the open tab bounces into a pill of its colour.
- **Chunky, toy-like buttons** sitting on a darker lip that sinks when pressed, plus pill-shaped chips, candy slider knobs and switches that turn green with a tick.
- **Rounded photo tiles** with little gaps, and each day in the galleries gets its own coloured label.
- **Settings sections in colour**, each with a coloured label and its icons on matching tiles.
- **Bouncing pictures** on the empty and waiting screens, and a confetti burst with the tick when posting is done.

## [0.6.4] - 2026-10-22 (build 14)

Light mode.

- **Light and dark, on every screen.** A sun / moon button in the top bar of every screen (the galleries, Selected, Upload, Settings, the viewer, the editor, publishing and setup) switches between the camera-screen dark and a warm paper-white light mode. The new colours spread out from the button in a circle, and nothing is lost: an edit in progress, the scroll position and anything picked stay as they were.
- **ShotDrop remembers it.** It opens in the mode you left it in. Dark is still the default.
- In light mode the yellow is a deeper gold, so buttons and icons stay readable on white; the viewer and the editor follow the mode too.

## [0.6.3] - 2026-10-22 (build 13)

A truthful crop, a sharper date filter, and a faster card.

- **Crop shows what you cropped.** After a small change to the frame (under a tenth of the photo), the crop view kept showing the previous crop squeezed into the new frame, so it looked wider and slimmer than what was saved. It now always shows exactly the part that will be saved. The view also no longer jumps when the hint under the photo changes from one line to two.
- **Filter by date and time of day** in both galleries: All Dates, Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, **One Date** or **Date Range**, together with **Morning** (5 AM–12 PM), **Afternoon** (12–5 PM), **Evening** (5–9 PM), **Night** (9 PM–5 AM) or your own **Time Range**. The button shows how many photos match before you apply it.
- **The card gallery fills in much faster.** Tiles use the preview the camera stores inside each JPEG (a few dozen KB) instead of reading the whole 10 MB photo, the photos on screen load before the ones scrolled past, and a full photo is read off the card once instead of up to three times. Coming back to ShotDrop, tiles still in memory show at once.
- **The editor opens faster.** A photo looked at in the viewer for a moment, or picked in the gallery, is copied off the card in the background, so Edit doesn't wait for it. While a photo is still coming off the card, the editor shows it straight away. Editing several photos, the next one is copied while you edit the current one.

## [0.6.2] - 2026-10-22 (build 12)

A crop you can see, and a date filter.

- **Crop stays where you leave it.** Let go of a corner and the frame stays put, showing just the cropped part at its place in the photo, ready to change again. (It used to zoom the crop up to fill the screen, which looked like the original photo.) Drag or pinch the photo to move it under the frame, as before.
- **Date filter** in both galleries: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or Pick Dates on a calendar. ✕ on the chip shows all dates again.
- **Thumbnails and downloads are cleared every time you leave ShotDrop,** even just to the home screen. An update being installed is kept until it's done.
- **"Did It Post?"** shows the Instagram logo with Facebook's beside each post (and the right one for a story or a Facebook post).
- "Plug In Your Camera Card" no longer has a Check Again button: the card is found by itself.

## [0.6.1] - 2026-10-22 (build 11)

A Phone Gallery, a cleaner crop, and a new icon.

- **Phone Gallery:** a new tab in the floating bar shows every photo on the phone (Android asks once). View, pick, edit and post them just like the card's. The card's tab is now the **SD Card Gallery**, and an SD Card | Phone switch at the top of each goes to the other. Unplugging the card still empties the SD Card Gallery; the Phone Gallery only opens when you go to it.
- **No more Choose Another Folder:** the SD Card Gallery shows the plugged-in card, and only the card.

- **"Did It Post?"** has just Yes and Do It Later.
- **New app icon:** your photo, filling the icon shape your launcher uses.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-10-22 (build 10)

Straight to Instagram, a real crop, and tidier storage.

- **Crop like Instagram.** The frame stays put in the middle; drag the photo to move it and pinch to zoom. What's outside the frame is cut off, and nothing gets squeezed. Drag a corner to change the frame itself.
- **Posts in the right shape.** Photos outside Instagram's 4:5 to 1.91:1 (like the D5600's 2:3 portraits), and carousels with mixed shapes, are sent in a shape Instagram takes. Pick Crop To Fit or Add Borders on the Publish sheet; the default is in Settings.
- **Start opens Instagram right away** with the photos and caption. "Did It Post?" comes up when you come back to ShotDrop, even if it was closed meanwhile. Yes sends the next post or story straight out; Do It Later keeps the photos in the Upload folder.
- **Instagram opening on its home feed:** when Instagram was closed in the background and opens without the photos, ShotDrop sends them again (with the caption helper on). Open It In Instagram does it by hand.
- **Opens when a card is plugged in,** on the Gallery. Android may ask once whether ShotDrop should always open for the card reader. Switch it off in Settings.
- **Storage looks after itself:** at launch and after publishing, ShotDrop clears copies nothing uses, copies of posted photos after a week (you choose: a day to never), old thumbnails, installed updates and old error notes. When the phone is low on space it tidies harder. Settings shows the space used and free, with Tidy Up Now.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-22 (build 6)

Four tabs, and a shortlist.

- A rounded bar floats at the bottom: **Gallery** (the card), **Selected**, **Upload** and **Settings**. Counts show on Selected and Upload; a dot on Settings when there's an update.
- **Selected** is your shortlist. Tap ♡ on a photo in the viewer, or long-press a few in the Gallery and tap "To Selected". ShotDrop copies them off the card, so they're there with the card unplugged. Tap one to edit it into the Upload folder.
- Photos in the Upload folder that you don't post go back to Selected afterwards, edits and all, ready for next time. Swiping one away in the Upload folder sets it aside in Selected too.
- If ShotDrop closes by itself, it leaves a note in Download (ShotDrop-crash-….txt) saying why, so it can be fixed.
- **Settings**: your Instagram account and caption auto-paste; the card folder; start portrait photos at 4:5; saved size (2160 or 1080 px); copy edits to the gallery; what Publish starts on; space used, and clearing posted copies and thumbnails; updates.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-22 (build 4)

Slim like Cymera's, and a smoother day-to-day.

**Card**
- Opens on the card's photos. The Instagram setup shows on the very first launch only, however you leave it.
- Plug in the card and its photos come up by themselves. A card used before opens straight away; a new card shows "Card found", and one tap opens the picker right on its DCIM folder (Android asks once per card).
- "Choose another folder" (⋮ menu) now really switches the grid to that folder. Rescanning updates it too.

**Editing**
- Crop, Body and Adjust are always there together: go from Slim straight to a filter or the crop and back, nothing to Apply first. Changes show as you make them; undo covers them all.
- **Slim**: tap Slim and pick the area, a line with a bracket at each end ("Select the waist area."). ShotDrop finds the body by itself (on the phone, offline) and starts the line on the waist with the brackets on the body's edges. Tap ✓, then slide: each bracket pushes its side of the body in (or out, for wider), as Cymera does. Earlier areas can be tapped and changed again, moved, or removed; "New area" adds another.
- **Stretch** goes both ways: taller or shorter.
- Re-cropping keeps Slim's areas and Stretch's lines on the same part of the body.
- Photos slimmed in 0.3.0 keep their Slim, as a line across the waist.

**Upload folder**
- Tick the photos to publish; unticked ones stay for later. Only the published ones leave the folder.
- Each photo has an Edit button (tapping the photo works too) to change its edit again from the original.

**Instagram**
- Caption auto-paste: switch on "ShotDrop caption paste" once (⋮ menu > Caption auto-paste walks you through it) and ShotDrop types the caption into Instagram's caption box itself. Off, it's copied for pasting as before.
- The "Added to the Upload folder" note now goes away after 3 seconds.

**Updates**
- ⋮ menu > Update app: checks for a new version, shows what's new, downloads it and opens Android's installer. A dot on the menu says when one is out.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-21 (build 3)

Body tools, Cymera-style.

- The Body tab has four tools, each opened on its own with a 0–100 slider and Cancel / Apply: **Stretch** (longer legs, taller look), **Slim** (draws the waist in), **Hip** (fuller hips), **Legs** (slimmer legs).
- ShotDrop finds the person in the photo by itself (on the phone, offline) and puts each tool in the right place: the waist for Slim, the hips for Hip, each leg for Legs, and Stretch's two lines from the hips to the ankles.
- Each tool's area shows as a dashed circle you can drag if it's off, or if no person is found.
- Replaces the old Taller/Shorter and Thinner/Wider sliders.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-21 (build 2)

Publishing.

- Publish from the Upload folder as **Post only**, **Post + Story** or **Story only**. ShotDrop opens Instagram's own post or story screen with the photos loaded, and you tap Share. Several photos become one carousel (10 at most per post).
- Story versions are made for you: 9:16, the photo at full width over a blurred copy of itself.
- The caption is copied when Instagram opens: long-press the caption box and tap Paste.
- A step-by-step screen: open Instagram, come back, say whether it posted. Posted photos leave the Upload folder, show POSTED in the grid, and their gallery copies move to Pictures/ShotDrop/Published.
- Facebook: Instagram can share every post and story to your Facebook profile by itself (Accounts Center). Or ShotDrop can open Facebook for each post too.
- New Instagram & Facebook screen, shown once on first launch and from the Upload folder: checks the apps are installed, remembers your username, and walks through turning on sharing to Facebook.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21 (build 1)

First build: card to Upload folder.

- Reads the D5600's SD card over USB OTG (pick the card's DCIM folder once; it opens by itself when the card is plugged in).
- Grid of every shot by day, JPG and NEF paired into one tile with a RAW badge; long-press to pick several.
- Full-screen viewer: swipe through the card, pinch or double-tap to zoom.
- "Edit before upload": crop (4:5, 1:1, 1.91:1, 9:16, free), rotate, flip, straighten; body reshape (taller / shorter, thinner / wider) with draggable bands; filters and brightness / contrast / saturation / warmth; undo / redo and hold to compare.
- Upload folder: edited photos in posting order (drag to reorder, swipe to remove with undo, tap to edit again from the original), caption, and a copy in Pictures/ShotDrop/Upload.
- Posting to Instagram comes next.
